What makes a useful community match review?

By Maya Singh, Community Editor · Published July 31, 2026

A strong match review separates observation from emotion. Fans can disagree about a manager, player or referee, but the discussion becomes more useful when each opinion includes a specific moment, tactical pattern or verified statistic.

Start with what happened

Describe the major phases of the contest without copying another publisher. Note changes in tempo, pressing, possession or shot selection, and explain why those changes mattered.

Credit every source

Statistics, quotes and photographs should have clear sources and valid usage rights. Never present rumors as confirmed facts. When a report is developing, label it clearly and update the article when reliable information changes.

Invite constructive disagreement

End with a focused question. Moderators should remove threats, hate speech, targeted harassment, spam and personal information while allowing good-faith criticism and competing opinions.
